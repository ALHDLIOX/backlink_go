## Full description

Groxen is a browser-based AI avatar generator for people who want a recognizable bot-style profile picture. Upload one clear selfie, pet photo, mascot, or character image in JPEG, PNG, or WebP format. The generator simplifies the face into flat colors, keeps identifying traits such as hair, glasses, or a hat, and returns a square icon with two black capsule eyes on a charcoal background. You can download the result or give instructions for another version. Each generation uses 10 credits; failed attempts are refunded. Groxen is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by xAI, Grok, or X Corp.
