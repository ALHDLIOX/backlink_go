## UTM policy

Website fields, canonical URL fields, and platforms requiring a canonical URL use the clean target:

```text
https://grokboticon.com/
```

When a platform permits a custom contextual link, use:

```text
https://grokboticon.com/?utm_source=<platform>&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=backlink
```

`<platform>` must be a stable lowercase ASCII identifier. No product-specific exception was supplied.

## Claims and positioning boundaries

Approved positioning:

- Independent browser-based AI tool that turns a clear photo of a person, pet, mascot, or character into a minimalist bot avatar.
- One consistent square icon style with black capsule eyes, with download and instruction-based editing as described on the [homepage](https://grokboticon.com/).

Do not claim:

- Any affiliation, sponsorship, endorsement, official status, or integration with xAI, Grok, X Corp., or third-party social platforms.
- Guaranteed resemblance, generation time, anonymity, privacy protection, upload rights, or platform acceptance of the output.
- Unlimited free generations, a fixed sign-up credit amount, subscriptions, discounts, or prices not checked on the submission day.
- Native iOS, Android, macOS, or Windows apps; general-purpose image editing; or features absent from the current public product.

Do not imply official recognition, certification, legal status, customer outcomes, exclusivity, revenue, usage scale, rankings, or performance without current explicit evidence in `product-profile.md`.

## Copy and taxonomy rules

- Preferred category: AI Avatar Generator.
- Allowed fallback categories: Profile Picture Maker; AI Image Generator; Creative Tools, when destination definitions fit.
- Prohibited or misleading categories: Official Grok App; xAI Product; X Integration; General Image Editor; Native Mobile App; Privacy Tool.
- Required boundary sentence where affiliation could be misunderstood: “Groxen is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by xAI, Grok, or X Corp.”
- Words, tones, or calls to action to avoid: Avoid “official,” “guaranteed,” “free forever,” “instant,” and implied endorsement. Use neutral calls such as “Create a bot avatar.”
