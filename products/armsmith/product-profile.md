## Full description (443 characters; recommended)

Armsmith is an AI coat of arms maker for families, couples, writers, and gaming communities. Follow four guided steps to choose an occasion, style, colors, shield, and symbols, then add a name, motto, or brief. Generate an original crest and save or download it from your private archive. Visual choices make it easy to shape a personal design. Armsmith creates original artwork; it does not grant official arms or certify historical heraldry.
