## UTM policy

Website fields, canonical URL fields, and platforms that require a canonical URL use the clean target:

```text
https://raumplaner.io/
```

When a platform permits a custom contextual link, use:

```text
https://raumplaner.io/?utm_source=<platform>&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=backlink
```

`<platform>` must be a stable lowercase ASCII identifier. No product-specific exception was supplied.
